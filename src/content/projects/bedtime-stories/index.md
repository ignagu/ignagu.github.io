Every evening, a new story. You give Claude a theme, a mood and a length, and it writes a short story designed to be read aloud at bedtime: simple sentences, a gentle arc, and a calm ending.

The Skill keeps the tone consistent from night to night, so stories feel like they come from the same storyteller.
