Most people have more recurring payments than they think: energy and water, phone and internet, insurance, streaming, software, apps, memberships. This project finds them all in your email, wherever the receipts land, and keeps one place where you can see them.

It builds a live dashboard from your past emails: what you pay each month, how each bill has moved over the last year, which subscriptions went up, and what renews or ends in the next 60 days. For energy and water it also shows whether a higher bill comes from using more or paying more per unit.

Every month it checks for new charges, spots new subscriptions you may have forgotten, flags price rises and late bills, and emails you a short report. It only reads and organises: it never pays, cancels or replies to anything. It works with any email service you have connected to Claude.
