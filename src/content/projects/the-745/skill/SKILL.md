# The 7:45

Produce a single page newspaper for today.

## Inputs
- Sections or topics (default: world, technology, science).
- Edition date in DD/MM/YYYY format (default: today).

## Layout
1. Masthead: "The 7:45" and the edition date.
2. Lead story: headline and about 150 words.
3. Three briefs: headline and two sentences each.
4. "One more thing": a short, thoughtful closing item.

## Rules
- Fit on one A4 page. Be concise.
- Use metric units and DD/MM/YYYY dates.
- Cite the source of every item in a short line at the bottom.
- Do not include any personal details about the reader.
