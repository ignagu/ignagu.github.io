Every evening, a new story. You tell Claude the listener's age, how long the story should be and what they love, and it writes a short story designed to be read aloud: simple sentences, a gentle arc, and a calm ending.

Stories change from night to night, and you can keep a recurring hero so they feel like they come from the same storyteller.
