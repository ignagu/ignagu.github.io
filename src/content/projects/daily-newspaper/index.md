A newspaper that fits on one page and takes five minutes to read. You pick the sections, and Claude writes a short edition: a lead story, a few briefs, and one thing worth thinking about. It works for one reader or a whole family, with every story kept suitable for the youngest reader.

It is designed to be read on a phone or printed on a single A4 page.
